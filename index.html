Information We Use ### Location Information The App may request access to your device's approximate or precise location in order to: - Calculate prayer times for your current location - Determine the Qibla direction - Select appropriate prayer time calculation settings - Display location-relevant prayer information Location information is used for the core functionality of the App. Where possible, prayer calculations are performed locally on your device. We do not operate our own server for the purpose of storing your location history. You may control location permissions through your device settings. ### Information Stored on Your Device The App may store certain preferences and application data locally on your device, including: - Prayer time calculation preferences - Notification preferences - Language and appearance settings - Prayer completion/check-in information - Last known location information where required for application functionality This information is used to provide and maintain App functionality. ## 2.
